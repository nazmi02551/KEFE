# ADR-0198: Historical Decision Retrospective Engine (CAP-028)

## Status

ACCEPTED

## Context

Societal wisdom expands by analyzing pivotal historical dilemmas (e.g. 1973 Oil Crisis energy rationing, 19th-century public health quarantine mandates, historical currency devaluations) under their contemporary constraints. Under `KEFE-PB-001`, `KEFE-ENG-001`, and `KEFE-ETG-001`, KEFE Retro presents historical cases with blind-first temporal isolation, allowing participants to weigh without knowing the retrospective hindsight bias.

## Decision

1. **Retrospective Epoch Model**:
   - `ANCIENT_CLASSICAL`: Pre-modern governance and ethical dilemmas.
   - `INDUSTRIAL_ERA`: 18th-19th century labor and technical transformations.
   - `TWENTIETH_CENTURY`: Modern geopolitics and civil rights milestones.
   - `CONTEMPORARY_CRISIS`: Recent financial and pandemic decisions.
2. **Hindsight Revelation**:
   - Compares user pre-revelation blind weigh against actual historical choice and downstream empirical consequence.

## Consequences

- Calibrates civic judgment against historical reality.
- Eliminates hindsight bias.
