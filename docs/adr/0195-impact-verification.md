# ADR-0195: Impact Verification & Milestone Outcome (CAP-054)

## Status

ACCEPTED

## Context

The final phase of democratic accountability is independent, multi-party Impact Verification. Under `KEFE-TIM-001`, `KEFE-ADM-001`, and `KEFE-PB-001`, once an institution delivers on an action pledge, community audit auditors, civil society panels, and domain experts evaluate whether the real-world outcome genuinely addressed the collective signal.

## Decision

1. **Verification Verdict Taxonomy**:
   - `FULL_RESOLUTION`: Core ethical grievance resolved and policy verified.
   - `SUBSTANTIAL_PROGRESS`: Material progress achieved with minor unaddressed items.
   - `PARTIAL_SYMBOLIC_ONLY`: Cosmetic gesture that failed to solve root structural harms.
   - `REJECTED_NON_COMPLIANT`: False or fraudulent claim of compliance.
2. **Impact Score**:
   - Normalized impact score ($0.0 - 1.0$) with peer-audit consensus score.

## Consequences

- Closes the accountability loop with verifiable, tamper-proof community auditing.
