# ADR-0167: Context Lens Neutral Background Engine (CAP-097)

## Status

ACCEPTED

## Context

Citizens deliberating on intricate public dilemmas often encounter sensationalized headlines without essential background context. To enable grounded reasoning without editorial manipulation, the platform needs a structured `ContextLens` that presents neutral factual, legal, and historical context prior to decision lock.

## Decision

1. **Governed Lens Pillars**:
   - `LEGAL_FRAMEWORK`: Governing statutes, international conventions, or constitutional provisions.
   - `HISTORICAL_CONTEXT`: Precedent cases and historical evolution of the issue.
   - `SCIENTIFIC_DATA`: Audited statistical baselines from recognized research bodies.
   - `COMPARATIVE_PRACTICE`: How other municipalities, jurisdictions, or societies approach the trade-off.
2. **Neutrality & Non-Directional Invariant**:
   - Background lenses must never advise a specific choice or endorse a normative preference.

## Consequences

- Dramatically elevates deliberative quality and factual comprehension.
- Preserves absolute platform neutrality and editorial rigor.
