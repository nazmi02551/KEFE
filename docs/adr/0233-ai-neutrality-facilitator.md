# ADR-0233: Neutrality-Guaranteed AI Deliberation Facilitator (CAP-093)

## Status

ACCEPTED

## Context

In high-heat community deliberations, human moderators can suffer from personal ideological fatigue or bias. Under `KEFE-PB-001`, `KEFE-ETG-001`, and `KEFE-ENG-001`, KEFE provides a Neutrality-Guaranteed AI Deliberation Facilitator that reframes toxic ad-hominem attacks into productive underlying value tensions while adhering to strict Socratic impartiality.

## Decision

1. **Facilitation Intervention Mode Taxonomy**:
   - `SOCRATIC_INQUIRY_PROMPT`: Posing clarifying value questions to deepen deliberation.
   - `NONVIOLENT_REFRAMING_SYNTHESIS`: Stripping vitriol while preserving substantive grievances.
   - `COMMON_GROUND_SURFACING`: Highlighting shared fundamental goals between opposing camps.
2. **Impartiality Invariant**:
   - AI facilitator must never endorse an outcome, rank moral worthiness, or take a substantive side.

## Consequences

- De-escalates destructive rhetoric without censoring substantive disagreement.
- Fosters empathetic cross-perspective deliberation.
