# ADR-0174: What Would Change My Mind Inquiry Engine (CAP-010)

## Status

ACCEPTED

## Context

In hyper-polarized discourse, decision-makers often adopt entrenched, unfalsifiable stances. Under `KEFE-CQB-001` (Content & Question Design Bible) and `KEFE-PB-001` (Product Bible), the system introduces a structured counterfactual inquiry asking: "Under what specific condition or evidence would you revise your position?"

## Decision

1. **Counterfactual Condition Taxonomy**:
   - `EMPIRICAL_DATA_THRESHOLD`: Quantitative shift in evidence or outcome.
   - `VULNERABILITY_PROTECTION`: Safeguards established for affected vulnerable groups.
   - `ECONOMIC_SUSTAINABILITY`: Viability and fiscal boundaries.
   - `MORAL_IMPASSE_EMPATHY`: Concrete evidence of disproportionate human suffering.
   - `UNCONDITIONAL_STANCE`: Absolute deontological or value-based conviction.
2. **Epistemic Flexibility Scoring**:
   - Classifies user response into: `CONDITIONALLY_FLEXIBLE`, `HIGHLY_EPISTEMIC_OPEN`, or `CATEGORICAL_ABSOLUTE`.

## Consequences

- Promotes epistemic humility without judging personal convictions.
- Provides deep cognitive clarity to the user in My KEFE reflection flows.
