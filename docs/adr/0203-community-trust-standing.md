# ADR-0203: Community Trust Score & Contribution Standing (CAP-070)

## Status

ACCEPTED

## Context

To encourage thoughtful, constructive participation and deter sybil attacks or spam brigades, the system calculates an objective Contribution Standing. Under `KEFE-PB-001`, `KEFE-SEC-001`, and `KEFE-TIM-001`, trust standing is strictly based on constructive deliberation behavior (high bridge resonance, accepted peer-reviewed proposals, adherence to community standards) without any ideological or partisan profiling.

## Decision

1. **Standing Tier Model**:
   - `EXEMPLARY_CONTRIBUTOR`: Highest civic resonance and verified contributions.
   - `ESTABLISHED_PARTICIPANT`: Consistent, high-integrity participation.
   - `ACTIVE_EXPLORER`: Standard onboarding tier.
   - `RESTRICTED_OR_PROBATIONARY`: Recent policy violations or active cooldown.
2. **Privacy Invariant**:
   - Scores evaluate behavioral integrity only; never ideological preference or voting alignment.

## Consequences

- Rewards high-quality deliberation.
- Protects democratic discourse from troll swarms.
