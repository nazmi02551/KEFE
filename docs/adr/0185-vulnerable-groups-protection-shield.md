# ADR-0185: Vulnerable Groups Protection Shield (CAP-026)

## Status

ACCEPTED

## Context

Policies that appear Pareto-optimal in aggregate may impose devastating, invisible costs on society's most fragile segments. In accordance with `KEFE-CQB-001` (Content & Question Design Bible), `KEFE-PB-001` (Product Bible), and the Rawlsian Maximin rule, every option must undergo a Vulnerable Cohort Protection audit.

## Decision

1. **Cohort Taxonomy**:
   - `CHILDREN_YOUTH`
   - `ELDERLY_GERIATRIC`
   - `LOW_INCOME_IMPOVERISHED`
   - `PERSONS_WITH_DISABILITIES`
   - `MINORITY_MARGINALIZED`
2. **Protection Classification**:
   - `STRONG_PROTECTIVE_FLOOR` (Affirmative safeguards).
   - `NEUTRAL_NO_DISPROPORTION` (No asymmetric harm).
   - `SEVERE_DISPROPORTIONATE_BURDEN` (Violates safety floor).

## Consequences

- Protects the worst-off members of society from majoritarian optimization blind spots.
- Embeds humanitarian ethics into civic weighing.
