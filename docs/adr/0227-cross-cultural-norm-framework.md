# ADR-0227: Cross-Cultural Norm Framework & Localized Values (CAP-081)

## Status

ACCEPTED

## Context

Normative ethical decisions (e.g., family autonomy, collective solidarity, individual privacy, environmental stewardship) are interpreted through varied cultural lenses across geographical regions without compromising universal human rights baselines. Under `KEFE-PB-001`, `KEFE-ETG-001`, and `KEFE-ENG-001`, KEFE provides a Cross-Cultural Norm Framework.

## Decision

1. **Cultural Norm Dimension Taxonomy**:
   - `COMMUNITY_SOLIDARITY_AND_MUTUALITY`: Communal resilience and social safety nets.
   - `INDIVIDUAL_AUTONOMY_AND_LIBERTY`: Personal sovereignty and self-determination.
   - `INTERGENERATIONAL_STEWARDSHIP`: Ecological continuity and ancestral legacy.
   - `PROCEDURAL_JUSTICE_AND_EQUITY`: Formal equality under deliberative law.
2. **Universal Rights Baseline Invariant**:
   - Cultural contextualization can never justify the abridgment of universal fundamental rights.

## Consequences

- Respects cultural pluralism and regional moral traditions.
- Unlocks global cross-border civic consensus without moral colonialism.
