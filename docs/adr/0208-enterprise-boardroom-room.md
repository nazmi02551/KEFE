# ADR-0208: Enterprise & Boardroom Decision Room (CAP-035)

## Status

ACCEPTED

## Context

Corporate boards, executive committees, and enterprise strategy teams face complex governance, ESG trade-offs, and capital allocation dilemmas. Under `KEFE-PB-001`, `KEFE-ETG-001`, and `KEFE-FIN-001`, KEFE provides a private Enterprise Decision Room with strict air-gapped encryption, weighted fiduciary voting, and stakeholder impact balancing.

## Decision

1. **Enterprise Scope Taxonomy**:
   - `ESG_AND_SUSTAINABILITY`: Climate compliance, supply chain ethics.
   - `CAPITAL_ALLOCATION_AND_MA`: Mergers, strategic acquisitions, resource bets.
   - `EXECUTIVE_COMPENSATION`: Performance pay, ethical equity distribution.
   - `CRISIS_MANAGEMENT`: Cybersecurity breaches, legal disputes, PR response.
2. **Fiduciary Invariant**:
   - Cryptographically isolated from public data feeds; zero data cross-leakage.

## Consequences

- Professional grade deliberation tool for corporate governance.
- High-integrity paper trail for regulatory fiduciary compliance.
