# ADR-0235: Explainable AI & Reasoning Provenance Graph (CAP-095)

## Status

ACCEPTED

## Context

Black-box AI recommendations are antithetical to democratic legitimacy. Under `KEFE-PB-001`, `KEFE-ETG-001`, and `KEFE-ENG-001`, KEFE provides an Explainable AI (XAI) & Reasoning Provenance Graph that renders the exact step-by-step causal chain, evidentiary weights, and constitutional axioms underlying every algorithmic deliberation synthesis.

## Decision

1. **Reasoning Transparency Tier Taxonomy**:
   - `FULL_AXIOMATIC_PROVENANCE`: Complete step-by-step logical derivation with primary source links.
   - `SIMPLIFIED_CAUSAL_TREE`: User-friendly dependency chain illustrating key trade-off pivots.
   - `EVIDENTIARY_WEIGHT_DECOMPOSITION`: Quantitative attribution breakdown of evidence inputs.
2. **Explainability Invariant**:
   - Zero black-box assertions: Every generated synthesis node must trace back to concrete user arguments or verified facts.

## Consequences

- Completely eliminates black-box opacity in algorithmic deliberation.
- Empowers citizens to inspect and audit machine reasoning.
