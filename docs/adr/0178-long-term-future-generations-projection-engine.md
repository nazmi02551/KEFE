# ADR-0178: Long-Term Future Generations Projection Engine (CAP-020)

## Status

ACCEPTED

## Context

Present-day decision-makers naturally suffer from present bias, discounting consequences beyond the immediate electoral or economic cycle. Under `KEFE-CQB-001` (Content & Question Design Bible) and `KEFE-PB-001` (Product Bible), every long-tail policy dilemma must model intergenerational externalities across multiple decades.

## Decision

1. **Multi-Decade Time Horizons**:
   - `HORIZON_5_YEARS` (Immediate implementation aftermath).
   - `HORIZON_20_YEARS` (One generation adulthood).
   - `HORIZON_50_YEARS` (Long-range structural equilibrium).
   - `HORIZON_100_YEARS` (Civilizational & climate sustainability).
2. **Intergenerational Equity Score**:
   - Aggregates ecological, debt, institutional, and human capital trajectories into a net intergenerational equity index in $[-1.0, 1.0]$.

## Consequences

- Gives voice to unborn generations in current civic weighing.
- Complies with constitutional sustainability mandates.
