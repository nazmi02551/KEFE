# ADR-0206: Education Mode & Civic Literacy Workshop (CAP-031)

## Status

ACCEPTED

## Context

To develop foundational critical thinking and democratic deliberation skills, KEFE provides an Education Mode. Under `KEFE-PB-001`, `KEFE-ETG-001`, and `KEFE-ENG-001`, this mode offers interactive curriculum modules, logical fallacy spotting drills, and structured dialectical exercises with non-punitive pedagogical feedback.

## Decision

1. **Literacy Module Taxonomy**:
   - `FALLACY_SPOTTING`: Identifying cognitive distortions in public rhetoric.
   - `ETHICAL_FRAMEWORKS`: Applying deontology, utilitarianism, and virtue ethics.
   - `EVIDENCE_EVALUATION`: Assessing source reliability and statistical claims.
   - `BRIDGE_SYNTHESIS`: Formulating high-resonance shared ground arguments.
2. **Mastery Progression**:
   - Tracks completed modules and comprehension score without storing sensitive psychological traits.

## Consequences

- Bridges theoretical ethics and real-world civic participation.
- Provides classrooms and civic groups with structured educational tools.
