# ADR-0242: Digital Sovereignty & Anti-Data-Colonialism Vault (CAP-110)

## Status

ACCEPTED

## Context

Citizens' civic, political, and deliberate data must not be extracted, monetized, or transferred across foreign jurisdictions by centralized tech monopolies. Under `KEFE-PB-001`, `KEFE-ETG-001`, and `KEFE-ENG-001`, KEFE establishes a Digital Sovereignty & Anti-Data-Colonialism Vault guaranteeing local data sovereignty, zero foreign telemetry exfiltration, and cryptographic jurisdictional residency.

## Decision

1. **Digital Sovereignty Tier Taxonomy**:
   - `SOVEREIGN_RESIDENCY_ENFORCED`: 100% cryptographic local vault storage; zero external foreign routing.
   - `CONTROLLED_EPHEMERAL_COMPUTE`: Ephemeral homomorphic enclave processing with immediate memory scrubbing.
   - `DATA_EXFILTRATION_BREACH_ALERT`: Unauthorized external exfiltration or cloud provider residency violation detected.
2. **Sovereignty Invariant**:
   - Civic deliberation artifacts and cryptographic vote receipts remain permanently under sovereign local citizen control.

## Consequences

- Completely protects national democratic deliberation from foreign surveillance and data harvesting.
- Sets the global benchmark for civic privacy and national digital self-determination.
