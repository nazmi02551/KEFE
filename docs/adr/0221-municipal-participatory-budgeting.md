# ADR-0221: Municipal & Participatory Budgeting (CAP-033)

## Status

ACCEPTED

## Context

Local municipalities and neighborhood councils require direct democratic mechanisms for allocating public capital expenditure while weighing trade-offs between competing urban infrastructure needs. Under `KEFE-PB-001`, `KEFE-ETG-001`, and `KEFE-ENG-001`, KEFE provides a Municipal Participatory Budgeting module supporting quadratic voting and district-level quota constraints.

## Decision

1. **Municipal Project Domain Taxonomy**:
   - `PARKS_AND_GREEN_SPACES`: Urban ecological infrastructure.
   - `PUBLIC_TRANSIT_AND_MOBILITY`: Sustainable micro-mobility and transit corridors.
   - `EDUCATION_AND_YOUTH_CENTERS`: Community libraries and youth centers.
   - `DISASTER_RESILIENCE_AND_SAFETY`: Seismic retrofitting and emergency preparation.
2. **Allocation Invariant**:
   - Total allocated fund cannot exceed available municipal fiscal envelope.

## Consequences

- Bridges citizen priorities directly to local municipal spending plans.
- Empowers neighborhood-level civic agency with transparent fiscal accountability.
