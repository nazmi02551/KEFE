# ADR-0210: Civil Society & NGO Impact Desk (CAP-037)

## Status

ACCEPTED

## Context

Civil society organizations, grassroots advocacy groups, and non-governmental entities need verified citizen consensus data and structured accountability tools to advance public advocacy and monitor institutional pledges. Under `KEFE-PB-001`, `KEFE-ETG-001`, and `KEFE-IMP-001`, KEFE provides a dedicated NGO Impact Desk.

## Decision

1. **Advocacy Domain Taxonomy**:
   - `HUMAN_RIGHTS_AND_JUSTICE`: Civil liberties, rule of law, anti-discrimination.
   - `ENVIRONMENT_AND_CLIMATE`: Habitat conservation, carbon reduction advocacy.
   - `PUBLIC_HEALTH_AND_SAFETY`: Healthcare access, community well-being.
   - `TRANSPARENCY_AND_ANTI_CORRUPTION`: Public spending audits, open governance.
2. **Impact Milestone Model**:
   - Tracks citizen consensus alignment, legislative petitions filed, and verified institutional policy reforms.

## Consequences

- Bridges grassroots civic energy with institutional legislative reform.
- Provides verifiable evidence of civil society efficacy.
