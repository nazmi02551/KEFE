# ADR-0241: Judicial Independence & Jurisprudential Consistency Chamber (CAP-109)

## Status

ACCEPTED

## Context

Judicial fairness requires statutory consistency, precedent fidelity, and immunity from political executive interference. Under `KEFE-PB-001`, `KEFE-ETG-001`, and `KEFE-ENG-001`, KEFE provides a Judicial Independence & Jurisprudential Consistency Chamber to analyze case precedent divergence, judicial discretion outliers, and rule-of-law baseline conformity.

## Decision

1. **Jurisprudential Consistency Status Taxonomy**:
   - `PRECEDENT_ALIGNED_CONSISTENT`: Rulings align with established constitutional jurisprudence and equality before law.
   - `DISCRETIONARY_DEVIATION_NOTICED`: Notable divergence from standard sentencing/precedent guidelines requiring justification.
   - `EXECUTIVE_INTERFERENCE_SUSPECTED`: Statistically anomalous deviation correlated with executive political pressure.
2. **Analytical Invariant**:
   - Evaluations assess statutory coherence and precedent variance without issuing legal verdicts or infringing on constitutional court authority.

## Consequences

- Surfaces systemic disparities and arbitrary precedent deviations.
- Strengthens public trust in the rule of law and equality before the judiciary.
