# ADR-0222: Dynamic Agenda Thresholding & Priority Surfacing (CAP-073)

## Status

ACCEPTED

## Context

Civic discourse agendas must emerge organically from broad, high-urgency grassroots citizen concerns rather than top-down media manipulation or algorithmic rage farming. Under `KEFE-PB-001`, `KEFE-ETG-001`, and `KEFE-ENG-001`, KEFE provides a Dynamic Agenda Thresholding engine that surfaces pressing dilemmas based on cross-demographic resonance, urgency velocity, and deliberate consensus potential.

## Decision

1. **Agenda Priority Tier Taxonomy**:
   - `NATIONAL_URGENCY_SPIKE`: Top-tier urgent issue crossing critical deliberative resonance threshold.
   - `REGIONAL_EMERGENT_TOPIC`: Medium-tier localized civic concern gathering momentum.
   - `MONITORED_INCUBATION`: Low-tier emerging topic under observation.
2. **Anti-Astroturfing Invariant**:
   - Resonant velocity requires geographic and viewpoint diversity to prevent coordinated bot flooding.

## Consequences

- Prevents artificially manufactured outrage from capturing the public agenda.
- Democratically surfaces authentic societal challenges with high urgency.
