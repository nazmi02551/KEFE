# ADR-0187: Principle-First Decision Flow Engine (CAP-006)

## Status

ACCEPTED

## Context

Under `KEFE-CQB-001`, `KEFE-PB-001`, and `KEFE-MPD-001`, users often make moral decisions based on emotional reaction to concrete scenarios rather than coherent principles. Principle-First Decision Flow asks users to rank/commit to abstract core principles (e.g. Individual Liberty, Collective Well-being, Procedural Justice, Compassion) *prior* to evaluating the concrete case details.

## Decision

1. **Core Principle Taxonomy**:
   - `INDIVIDUAL_LIBERTY`: Primacy of autonomy and individual consent.
   - `COLLECTIVE_WELLBEING`: Maximizing overall utility and public health/safety.
   - `PROCEDURAL_JUSTICE`: Strict adherence to established rules, rule of law, and due process.
   - `EMPATHY_COMPASSION`: Minimizing suffering and protecting vulnerable entities.
2. **Principle-Case Consistency**:
   - Compare user's pre-committed principle priority against their final verdict to reveal moral consistency.

## Consequences

- Fosters metacognitive reflection on philosophical foundations.
- Neutral and non-judgmental presentation.
