# ADR-0152: Action Proposal and Milestone Follow-through (CAP-051)

## Status

ACCEPTED

## Context

Following community deliberation (ME & WE) and qualified Signal generation, civic impact requires actionable follow-through. Statements and promises without auditable milestone tracking risk dissipating public trust.

## Decision

1. **Structured Action Entity**:
   - Linked to a specific CaseVersion / Signal and optional parent Institution Response.
   - Status progression: `PROPOSED` $\rightarrow$ `IN_PROGRESS` $\rightarrow$ `VERIFIED_COMPLETE` (or `STALLED`).
2. **Audited Evidence & Progress Tracking**:
   - Actions support progress metrics (`progress_percentage`: 0-100), target completion dates, and verifiable evidence references (`evidence_summary`, `evidence_url`).
3. **Neutrality & Verification Boundary**:
   - Action verification requires explicit verification criteria and authority provenance; KEFE maintains platform neutrality without taking normative advocacy stances.

## Consequences

- Bridges the gap between collective consensus and tangible real-world reform.
- Establishes a transparent milestone accountability layer.
