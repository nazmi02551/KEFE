# ADR-0177: Cognitive Fallacy and Distortion Detector (CAP-042)

## Status

ACCEPTED

## Context

In public deliberation, informal logical fallacies frequently derail constructive civic weighing. In accordance with `KEFE-CQB-001` (Content & Question Design Bible) and `KEFE-TIM-001` (Trust & Integrity), KEFE provides real-time detection of rhetorical distortions to protect community reasoning from manipulative fallacies.

## Decision

1. **Fallacy Taxonomy**:
   - `AD_HOMINEM`: Personal attack ignoring argument merit.
   - `STRAW_MAN`: Exaggerated or caricature distortion of opposing view.
   - `FALSE_DILEMMA`: Artificially binary framing ignoring third alternatives.
   - `SLIPPERY_SLOPE`: Unwarranted catastrophic chain-reaction assertions.
   - `APPEAL_TO_EMOTION_FEAR`: Manipulative visceral fearmongering.
   - `NO_FALLACY_DETECTED`: Clear, valid reasoning.
2. **Pedagogical Feedback**:
   - Instead of shaming or censoring, the detector offers constructive explanations to help users strengthen their reasoning.

## Consequences

- Elevates the quality of civic reasoning across society.
- Fulfills the intellectual growth promise of KEFE.
