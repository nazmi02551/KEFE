# ADR-0240: Institutional Promise & Outcome Realization Matrix (CAP-108)

## Status

ACCEPTED

## Context

Governments and institutions regularly make public commitments during civic consultations, but citizens rarely have a structured longitudinal mechanism to verify whether promised outcomes were actually delivered. Under `KEFE-PB-001`, `KEFE-ETG-001`, and `KEFE-ENG-001`, KEFE provides an Institutional Promise & Outcome Realization Matrix tracking milestone fulfillment, target delivery dates, and empirical evidence proofs.

## Decision

1. **Promise Realization Status Taxonomy**:
   - `PROMISE_DELIVERED_VERIFIED`: Measurable targets fulfilled with verified empirical proof.
   - `IN_PROGRESS_ON_TRACK`: Active milestone progress within statutory deadline.
   - `PROMISE_BROKEN_DEFAULT`: Deadline elapsed without verifiable delivery.
2. **Accountability Invariant**:
   - Institutional historical fidelity scores directly influence the institutional endorsement weighting across future policy simulations.

## Consequences

- Holds elected officials and municipal authorities durably accountable to civic commitments.
- Closes the democratic loop from deliberation to real-world policy execution.
