# ADR-0223: Citizen Jury & Sortition Deliberation Chamber (CAP-046)

## Status

ACCEPTED

## Context

Lottocracy / Sortition (random stratified citizen selection) creates mini-publics that deliberate on intricate policy dilemmas with balanced expert testimony. Under `KEFE-PB-001`, `KEFE-ETG-001`, and `KEFE-ENG-001`, KEFE provides a Citizen Jury Chamber that empanels statistically stratified citizen juries to weigh evidence, cross-examine expert witnesses, and publish binding civic verdicts.

## Decision

1. **Jury Mandate Stage Taxonomy**:
   - `STRATIFIED_PANEL_ASSEMBLY`: Random demographic quota empanelment.
   - `EXPERT_HEARINGS_IN_SESSION`: Synchronous / asynchronous expert testimony review.
   - `CONSENSUS_VERDICT_EMITTED`: Published supermajority deliberative policy verdict.
2. **Sortition Invariant**:
   - Stratified random selection guarantees demographic balance without ideological bias or quota skew.

## Consequences

- Restores representative democratic mini-public deliberation.
- Fosters high-trust civic verdicts decoupled from party-line polarization.
