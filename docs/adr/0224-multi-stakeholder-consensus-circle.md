# ADR-0224: Multi-Stakeholder Consensus Circle & Synthesis (CAP-047)

## Status

ACCEPTED

## Context

Polarized socio-economic disputes (e.g., labor vs automation, environmental protection vs industrial output) require structured round-table consensus circles where diverse stakeholder representatives can formulate synthesized compromise pacts. Under `KEFE-PB-001`, `KEFE-ETG-001`, and `KEFE-ENG-001`, KEFE provides a Multi-Stakeholder Consensus Circle engine tracking mutual concession indices and synthesis pact integrity.

## Decision

1. **Consensus Circle State Taxonomy**:
   - `STAKEHOLDER_DIAMETRIC_IMPASSE`: High friction, zero mutual concessions.
   - `INTERMEDIATE_CONCESSION_BARGAINING`: Active trade-off exchanges underway.
   - `SYNTHESIS_PACT_RATIFIED`: Multi-party signed compromise covenant.
2. **Equity Invariant**:
   - Synthesis pact must include positive net utility for all registered stakeholder groups.

## Consequences

- Replaces zero-sum partisan hostility with positive-sum deliberative synthesis.
- Produces durable, multi-party policy compromises.
