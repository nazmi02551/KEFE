# ADR-0202: Appeals & Community Review Panel (CAP-069)

## Status

ACCEPTED

## Context

When a user's argument, objection, or proposal is moderated or rejected, procedural justice requires an independent appeal mechanism. Under `KEFE-CQB-001`, `KEFE-ADM-001`, and `KEFE-PB-001`, an appeal is submitted to a randomly selected, multi-stakeholder Community Review Panel to prevent entrenched editorial bias.

## Decision

1. **Appeal Verdict Taxonomy**:
   - `OVERTURNED_RESTORED`: Moderation decision reversed; content fully restored.
   - `UPHELD_VIOLATION_CONFIRMED`: Moderation decision confirmed with final explanation.
   - `PARTIAL_REVISION_PERMITTED`: User permitted to edit and resubmit sanitized version.
2. **Panel Consensus**:
   - Requires supermajority ($\ge 67\%$) of panel members.

## Consequences

- Democratic checks and balances on moderation.
- Protects controversial but constructive minority perspectives.
