# ADR-0163: Divergence Anatomy Breakdown Engine (CAP-040)

## Status

ACCEPTED

## Context

When voters reach divergent conclusions, they often assume their opponents are ill-intentioned or misinformed. In reality, deliberation reveals that divergence typically arises from distinct, legitimate causal roots: fundamental value weighting, factual probability estimates, procedural governance preferences, or time-horizon discounting.

## Decision

1. **Taxonomy of Divergence Drivers**:
   - `NORMATIVE_VALUE_WEIGHT`: Differential weighting of legitimate principles (e.g. Security vs Privacy).
   - `FACTUAL_PROBABILITY_ASSESSMENT`: Differing empirical estimates of risk or outcome likelihood.
   - `PROCEDURAL_GOVERNANCE`: Disagreement on jurisdiction, enforcement mechanism, or oversight.
   - `TIME_HORIZON`: Prioritizing immediate alleviation versus long-term systemic sustainability.
2. **Deconstructed Driver Breakdown**:
   - Computes the percentage contribution of each driver to the observed collective divergence.

## Consequences

- Replaces partisan hostility with structural cognitive clarity.
- Helps deliberators understand the precise root of disagreement.
