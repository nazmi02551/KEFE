# ADR-0238: Revolving Door & Political Transition Monitor (CAP-106)

## Status

ACCEPTED

## Context

When senior regulatory officials move immediately into lucrative executive roles within the industries they previously regulated (or vice-versa), public policies become vulnerable to regulatory capture. Under `KEFE-PB-001`, `KEFE-ETG-001`, and `KEFE-ENG-001`, KEFE provides a Revolving Door Monitor tracking public-to-private employment transitions, cooling-off compliance, and regulatory capture risk.

## Decision

1. **Revolving Door Transition Status Taxonomy**:
   - `COOLING_OFF_COMPLIANT`: Full adherence to statutory post-employment cooling-off intervals.
   - `RAPID_REGULATORY_TRANSITION_ALERT`: Unbuffered transition between high-stakes regulation and regulated sector.
   - `PROVEN_CAPTURE_VULNERABILITY`: Direct policy conflict identified during active tenure.
2. **Transparency Invariant**:
   - Regulatory histories of institutional witnesses and policy proposal authors are visible to deliberators.

## Consequences

- Discourages corrupt regulatory capture and quid-pro-quo appointments.
- Equips citizens with objective background context on institutional actors.
