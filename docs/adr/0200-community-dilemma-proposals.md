# ADR-0200: Community Dilemma Proposals & Transparent Curation (CAP-030)

## Status

ACCEPTED

## Context

A decentralized, grassroots platform must empower communities to propose new ethical and policy dilemmas without gatekeeping, while enforcing strict editorial neutrality and anti-harassment safeguards. Under `KEFE-CQB-001` (Editorial Quality Bible), `KEFE-ADM-001`, and `KEFE-PB-001`, Community Dilemma Proposals formalizes user submission, multi-editor triage, and transparent rejection reasoning.

## Decision

1. **Proposal Curation Lifecycle**:
   - `DRAFT_SUBMITTED`: Initial proposal from authenticated or pseudo-anonymous user.
   - `COMMUNITY_PEER_REVIEW`: Open review for neutrality and clarity.
   - `EDITORIAL_APPROVED`: Conforms to editorial standards, promoted to publishable CaseVersion.
   - `REJECTED_WITH_REASON`: Neutrality or privacy violation with published rejection notice.
2. **Quality Metrics**:
   - Objective neutrality score ($0.0 - 1.0$) and supporter endorsement count.

## Consequences

- Democratizes the agenda-setting process.
- Prevents weaponized, biased, or leading dilemma formulations.
