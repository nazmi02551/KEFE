# ADR-0232: Synthetic Argument & Astroturfing Bot Shield (CAP-092)

## Status

ACCEPTED

## Context

Generative AI swarms and coordinated astroturfing campaigns can artificially flood public deliberation with thousands of synthetically generated variations of a single partisan talking point to manipulate collective consensus metrics. Under `KEFE-PB-001`, `KEFE-ETG-001`, and `KEFE-ENG-001`, KEFE provides an Astroturfing Bot Shield utilizing semantic clustering entropy and proof-of-humanity verification.

## Decision

1. **Sybil / Bot Cluster State Taxonomy**:
   - `ORGANIC_CITIZEN_AUTHENTIC`: Natural semantic diversity across human contributors.
   - `SUSPECTED_BOT_COORDINATION`: High syntactic repetition and synchronized arrival timing.
   - `ISOLATED_QUARANTINE_SWARM`: Automated quarantine blocking collective metric poisoning.
2. **Quarantine Invariant**:
   - Quarantined bot submissions are isolated from collective consensus aggregation without alerting malicious swarm operators.

## Consequences

- Neutralizes AI-powered astroturfing and Sybil attacks.
- Protects democratic signal integrity and authentic human consensus.
