# ADR-0244: Intergenerational Justice & Planetary Rights Proxy (CAP-112)

## Status

ACCEPTED

## Context

Short-term political horizons systematically disadvantage unborn future generations and planetary ecosystems that cannot cast votes. Under `KEFE-PB-001`, `KEFE-ETG-001`, and `KEFE-ENG-001`, KEFE establishes an Intergenerational Justice & Planetary Rights Proxy to model the 50-year horizon impact, ecological boundary thresholds, and future generation stewardship burden of policies.

## Decision

1. **Intergenerational Impact Status Taxonomy**:
   - `REGENERATIVE_FUTURE_STEWARDSHIP`: Net-positive long-term planetary replenishment and generational equity.
   - `TRANSITIONAL_BURDEN_MONITORED`: Temporary intergenerational trade-off with enforceable mitigation milestones.
   - `INTERGENERATIONAL_DEBT_DEPLETION`: Severe depletion transferring catastrophic ecological or economic liabilities to youth/future cohorts.
2. **Horizon Invariant**:
   - Every policy decision evaluates a minimum 50-year planetary boundary impact simulation.

## Consequences

- Gives structural deliberative voice to future generations and ecological systems.
- Prevents short-term populism from externalizing existential costs onto youth.
