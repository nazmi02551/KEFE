# ADR-0207: Youth & Student Deliberation Space (CAP-032)

## Status

ACCEPTED

## Context

Youth and student demographics often encounter barriers to formal institutional decision-making. Under `KEFE-PB-001`, `KEFE-ETG-001`, and `KEFE-ENG-001`, KEFE introduces dedicated Youth & Student Deliberation Spaces designed around campus policies, climate futures, and educational equity dilemmas with age-appropriate safety guards.

## Decision

1. **Space Focus Taxonomy**:
   - `CAMPUS_AND_EDUCATION_POLICY`: University governance, grading, curriculum.
   - `CLIMATE_AND_INTERGENERATIONAL`: Long-term environmental sustainability.
   - `DIGITAL_RIGHTS_AND_AI`: Student privacy, AI in academia, online liberties.
   - `CIVIC_ENTREPRENEURSHIP`: Grassroots youth community projects.
2. **Safety Invariants**:
   - Zero behavioral advertising, no data broker sharing, enhanced bullying/harassment moderation shields.

## Consequences

- Fosters healthy youth engagement in public affairs.
- Provides educational institutions with verified student consensus data.
